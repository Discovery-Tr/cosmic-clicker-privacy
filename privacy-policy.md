# Privacy Policy for Cosmic Clicker

Last updated: November 5, 2026

## Introduction
Cosmic Clicker ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information about you when you use our mobile game Cosmic Clicker.

## Information We Collect
We collect the following information:
- **Player name**: The username you choose when playing the game
- **Game scores**: Your high scores stored on our servers
- **Device information**: Basic device data collected by third-party services

## How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:
- Display your score on the global leaderboard
- Improve the game experience
- Show relevant advertisements

## Third Party Services
Our app uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

### Google AdMob
We use Google AdMob to show advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data for personalized advertising. For more information, visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Google Firebase
We use Firebase Firestore to store leaderboard scores. For more information, visit: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## Data Retention
Player names and scores are stored on Firebase servers. You can request deletion of your data by contacting us.

## Children's Privacy
Cosmic Clicker is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Security
We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
